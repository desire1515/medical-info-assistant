The Problem

Finding reliable next steps can be difficult

In Malawi, gaps in accessible health information can make it harder to know where to seek care. Unverified information may encourage self-diagnosis, while delaying contact with a qualified health worker can be unsafe.

  • Health information can be difficult to navigate.
  • Unverified information may encourage self-diagnosis.
  • Uncertainty can make it harder to choose a next step.

The Solution

Simple rules, clear safety boundaries

This prototype uses a small, reviewable rule set to offer general care-navigation guidance. An emergency safety gate is checked first, recommendations explain their rule, and every page makes the limits clear.
