About this Project

A small client-side system demonstrating transparent, rule-based health information and navigation.

System overview

How it works

The portfolio is built with semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. The assistant uses JavaScript to read a JSON-style structured knowledge base, compare selected options with predefined rules, and render the matching general guidance. It does not use a server, framework, or machine-learning model.

Data sources & references

The simplified rules in this prototype are predefined for demonstration and are not a clinical guideline. For general health information and public guidance, consult the World Health Organization and the Malawi Ministry of Health through their official homepages.

Malawi context

Local resources (Malawi)

People may contact a Health Surveillance Assistant or local health worker, or visit a health centre or district hospital. Chipatala Cha Pa Foni may be available in some locations. Availability may vary — confirm with official sources.

Full disclaimer

This educational prototype provides general health information and care-navigation suggestions only. It is not a medical device, does not provide a diagnosis, and is not a substitute for an individual assessment or advice from a qualified health worker. Its rules are simplified and may not cover your situation. Do not delay seeking urgent or emergency care to use this website. If you are concerned, contact a qualified local health worker or visit a health facility. No personal health information is stored or transmitted by this site.

ⓘ

Important: This educational tool provides general information only. It does not diagnose or replace a qualified health worker. In an emergency, seek care at your nearest health facility immediately.
