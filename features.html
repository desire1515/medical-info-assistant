Features & Live Demo

A focused tour of the assistant’s transparent rules, safety-first flow, and design.

Built around clarity

What the tool does

Transparent rule engine

Predefined conditions are evaluated in a clear, reviewable priority order.

Emergency safety gate

Any selected emergency warning sign overrides every other rule.

Explainable recommendations

Each result shares the reason associated with its matching rule.

No diagnosis, no prescriptions

The tool only provides general navigation and does not suggest conditions or medicines.

Mobile-first design

Responsive layouts and keyboard-accessible controls support use across screen sizes.

No personal data stored

Selections stay in the current browser page and are not saved or sent to a server.

Safety before matching

How the safety gate works

01

User selects symptoms

Selections include emergency warning signs and general symptoms.

02

Emergency rule is checked first

If any red-flag symptom is selected, the emergency result overrides all other rules.

03

Other rules are compared

Otherwise, matches are evaluated by priority and the top match is shown.

Result categories

The four urgency levels

EMERGENCY

Seek urgent care at the nearest health facility now.

SEE A CLINICIAN SOON

Contact a health worker soon for an assessment (within 1–2 days as described by this prototype).

ROUTINE APPOINTMENT

Arrange a routine appointment when convenient.

GENERAL GUIDANCE

Monitor your symptoms and consult a health worker if concerned.

Urgency labels are general navigation categories, not a clinical triage assessment. Do not delay emergency care to use this tool.
